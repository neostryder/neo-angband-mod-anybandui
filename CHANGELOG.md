# Changelog

All notable changes to this mod are recorded here. Versions follow the mod's own `manifest.json`, which is what the game reads, and each released version has a matching git tag that an install pins itself to.

An entry has to matter to somebody running the mod. Documentation wording, internal refactoring and test-only additions are not recorded here. Bug fixes are, however small.

An entry opens with one or more bracketed tags. `[Visible]` marks a change a player would notice in the game or mod itself; `[Internal]` marks one that touches only code, tooling, or a maintainer's own workflow, with nothing for a player to see. A further tag (`[Security]`, `[Balance]`, `[UI]`, `[Modding-API]`, `[Localization]`, `[Save-Compat]`, `[Docs]`, `[Content]`, `[Compatibility]`, and others as they come up) names what kind of change it is. Lists appear in this order and each is omitted when empty for a release: Added, Changed, Removed, Fixed.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- [Visible] [UI] **Optional visual effects for the dungeon view.** They include a CRT look with scanlines, a red tint at low health, a burst at death, a glow on known artifacts, marks over sleeping creatures, a haze around uniques, and rings for hits, misses, spells, healing and teleports. Each has its own switch and starts off. They sit over text and tiles alike and follow the zoom and camera, and animated ones hold still when your system asks for reduced motion.
- [Visible] [UI] **A store window with prices and equipment comparison.** In a store or your home it shows the stock beside your pack, with prices, and compares anything wearable with what you have on. Buying and selling from its buttons waits for a newer version of the game. Each part has its own switch and starts off.
- [Visible] [UI] **A spell panel, a thirty-slot quickbar and a rest panel.** The spell panel lists each carried book's spells with mana, fail rate and description, and casts or studies with a click. The quickbar puts spells, potions, scrolls, wands and activations on the number keys, with Shift and Ctrl rows, and each character keeps its own slots. The rest panel rests until you recover or for a set number of turns. Each has its own switch and starts off.
- [Visible] [UI] **An item panel with search, inspection and equipment comparison.** It lists your pack and equipment, shows the game's own description of the selected item, and compares your equipment with the change before you wear something. It also answers the game's quantity and item questions with buttons, marks new items, and offers buttons to wield, drop, inscribe or use the selected item. Each part has its own switch and starts off.
- [Visible] [UI] **Click to walk, a right-click menu for each tile, and a gold aiming path.** A left-click steps or travels to a tile, attacks a creature next to you, or picks the target while you aim. A right-click lists what you can do on that tile. While you aim, a gold line shows where the shot would go. Each has its own switch and starts off.
- [Visible] [UI] **Zoom and pan, hover cards and a full-level map overview, brought over from Quality of Life.** Zoom keeps whole cells so tiles stay crisp, and it can also follow your character, pan with a mouse drag, keep the aiming cursor on screen and centre on each new level. Hover cards work on the dungeon view and the map overview with one shared delay. The map overview adds an outline of the main view, landmark markers with a legend, mouse zoom and pan, and a flat-colour schematic mode. Each feature has its own switch and starts off.
- [Visible] [UI] **High-contrast and colourblind displays, first-encounter alerts, an itemized quiver and sharper shrunken tiles, brought over from Quality of Life.** Each has its own switch and starts off. The two display filters also cover this mod's panels.
- [Visible] [UI] **Panels for the character column, the status line and the message line.** Each has its own switch on the Mods screen and starts off. The character card shows bars for hit points, spell points, food and experience beside your stats, gold, armour and speed. The status panel shows a badge for each timed effect and a card for the current level, and the message log folds repeated messages and can be searched.
- [Internal] **The mod repository, with a plugin that loads and adds nothing to the screen.** It declares no capabilities and logs one line when the game loads it.
