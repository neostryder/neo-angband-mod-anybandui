# Changelog

All notable changes to this mod are recorded here. Versions follow the mod's own `manifest.json`, which is what the game reads, and each released version has a matching git tag that an install pins itself to.

An entry has to matter to somebody running the mod. Documentation wording, internal refactoring and test-only additions are not recorded here. Bug fixes are, however small.

An entry opens with one or more bracketed tags. `[Visible]` marks a change a player would notice in the game or mod itself; `[Internal]` marks one that touches only code, tooling, or a maintainer's own workflow, with nothing for a player to see. A further tag (`[Security]`, `[Balance]`, `[UI]`, `[Modding-API]`, `[Localization]`, `[Save-Compat]`, `[Docs]`, `[Content]`, `[Compatibility]`, and others as they come up) names what kind of change it is. Lists appear in this order and each is omitted when empty for a release: Added, Changed, Removed, Fixed.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- [Visible] [UI] **More combat and movement effects: fear marks, damage numbers, walking slides, a teleport ripple, bolt trails and breath cones.** A frightened creature shows a `!` where a sleeping one shows its mark. Damage floats up red over your character and gold over a creature, and healing floats up green. A creature you can see slides into the square it steps to, and a ripple spreads from where a teleport lands. A bolt leaves a trail that points the way it flew, and a breath washes outward from where it starts. Each has its own switch and strength and starts off, and the moving ones hold still when your system asks for reduced motion.
- [Visible] [UI] **Embers, a recall ring, a level flash, scene fades and more of the CRT look.** Embers glow over lava and stairs you have seen, a ring circles your character while Word of Recall counts down, and your square flashes the first time you pass your highest level this session. The old view fades out when a character loads, when you take stairs or enter a shop, and when you die. The CRT look gains phosphor glow, colour fringing and a band of light that sweeps down the view, in text mode and with tiles. Each has its own switch and strength setting and starts off, and the moving ones hold still or shorten when your system asks for reduced motion.
- [Visible] [UI] **A character sheet, an equipment portrait and a theme editor.** The character sheet opens in its own panel with the panels, seven-column stat breakdown and flag grid of the game's character screen, plus the background. The equipment tab gains a Figure view that hangs each worn item beside the part of an ASCII body it covers. The theme editor sets the background, surface, text and accent colours and the corner rounding, can invert the dungeon view, and saves the result as your custom theme.
- [Visible] [UI] **The message log filters and keeps every line.** All, Combat, Loot and Other sort the log by message type, the log scrolls back through every message instead of the last 20, and each repeated line shows its count. A message cut short ends in three dots, and the waiting-messages and resting signs are drawn as a small emblem.
- [Visible] [UI] **A quantity slider and a spell browser in the stores.** Buying more than one item gives a slider beside the amount, and OK stays off while the exact total costs more gold than you carry. A spellbook in stock has a Browse spells button that lists its spells with your own chance of failure.
- [Visible] [UI] **Character cards, a graveyard, a run journal and a Hall of Fame table.** Your saves show as cards on the character select screen with race, class, level and depth, and you can continue, rename or delete one from there. When a run ends a post-mortem opens with your last messages, journal, belongings and character sheet, and the graveyard keeps it after the save is gone. The run journal lists level-ups, new depths, unique kills and artifacts, and the game's yes or no questions open in a dialog with Yes and No buttons. Each has its own switch and starts off.
- [Visible] [UI] **A character creation wizard.** Race, class, attributes, name and background each get their own step in one window, then a review, and a preview beside each step shows the stats, hit points and starting gold the game will give you. Point buy, random rolls and reusing your previous character all work, and Escape goes back a step. It has its own switch and starts off.
- [Visible] [UI] **A knowledge browser, a game options editor, a keybinding editor and a settings window.** The knowledge browser opens the recall page of anything your character knows, from monsters and artifacts to runes and traps. The options editor changes the interface options, the hit point warning and the delays, and the keybinding editor tells you what a key runs before you bind it. The settings window gathers this mod's theme, hover delay and effect strengths in one place. Each has its own switch and starts off.
- [Visible] [UI] **A death recap.** When your character dies, a recap shows what killed it, the last blows and healing it took, its final messages, and its depth, level, turns and latest milestones. With the graveyard on it is the first page of the post-mortem, and it names the autoplayer if one was playing. It has its own switch and starts off.
- [Visible] [UI] **Pickup previews.** Items on the floor where you stand get the item panel's equipment comparison before you pick them up, along with how many of that kind you already carry. Hovering one shows the comparison in a single line, and so does the dungeon hover card for an item you can see. It has its own switch and starts off.
- [Visible] [UI] **Map travel.** Clicking a square you remember on the level map sends your character there by the game's own travel, which stops when something disturbs you. It has its own switch and starts off.
- [Visible] [UI] **Two theme packs.** Linoleum, dark and warm with cyan highlights, and Plain terminal, grey on black with square corners and no decorative marks, join the theme presets in the theme panel and the settings window. They have their own switch and start off.

### Changed

- [Visible] [UI] **An artifact on the floor glows with a gold sunburst instead of a circle.**
- [Visible] [UI] **Hovering on the map overview waits for the shared hover delay instead of two seconds.**
- [Internal] [Modding-API] **AnybandUI reads the game through one file, src/host-adapter.ts.** Features used to call the engine core, the live state and the input snapshot themselves. Now they call functions in that file, each named for the mod API 2 call that will take its place, so porting to API 2 means rewriting one file. The real-map read behind the hidden-magic glow has its own function, separate from what the character knows, and a new test fails if any other file reaches into the host. Play is the same as before.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-10-01

### Added

- [Visible] [UI] **Glow shows hidden magic, a new switch that works with the floor item glow.** Turn it on and a cursed item, an artifact or an item with runes glows on the floor before you have identified it, matching the native AnybandUI. It starts off because it gives away what the game hides.

### Changed

- [Visible] [UI] **The cards, the map menus and the window frames around them take on Angband's own look.** They use the game's 8x13 dialog font and a palette close to its terminal, with thin borders, a coloured band under each heading and small marks in each card's corners. While any card is on, the frames around every pane change to the same colours, so the game's own subwindows and the cards read as one screen. Turning every card off gives the frames the game's usual look back.
- [Visible] [UI] **The dungeon card shows a dash instead of a question mark while the level feeling is not known yet.**
- [Visible] [Compatibility] **AnybandUI now needs Neo Angband 1.21.1 or later.** It is built and tested against that version.

### Removed

- [Internal] **Removed this repo's own Discord release announcer.** Its workflow, script and test are gone. The releases site at releases.rpgm.tools now posts each new release to the Neo Angband announcements forum.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-09-27

### Added

- [Visible] [UI] **Optional visual effects for the dungeon view.** They include a CRT look with scanlines, a red tint below the game's hit point warning, a burst at death, glows on floor items that are artifacts, cursed or carry an unknown rune, marks over sleeping creatures, a haze around uniques, rings for hits, misses, spells, healing and teleports, and flashes where a ball or breath lands. Each has its own switch and starts off. They sit over text and tiles alike and follow the zoom and camera, and animated ones hold still when your system asks for reduced motion. On a version of the game with numeric mod settings, each effect also has a strength setting on the Mods screen, from 0% to 100%.
- [Visible] [UI] **The item, spell, quickbar and rest cards open as panes in the game's own window layout.** On a game version that tiles mod panels, each card docks, tabs and resizes with the dungeon view and the subwindows, and the layout remembers where you put it. The quickbar docks along the bottom and sizes itself to its rows. On an older version the cards open over the game as before. With zoom on, Ctrl-Shift with the zoom keys now scales these cards too, from 75 to 150 percent.
- [Visible] [UI] **A store window where you can buy, sell, stash and compare.** In a store or your home it shows the stock beside your pack with prices, greys out what the store will not take or you cannot afford, and compares anything wearable with each slot it could go in. Buying and selling go through the game's own questions about how many and at what price. On an older version of the game the window only shows the stock. Each part has its own switch and starts off.
- [Visible] [UI] **A spell panel, a thirty-slot quickbar, a rest panel and a blast preview.** The spell panel lists each carried book's spells with mana, fail rate and description, says how many new spells you can learn, and casts or studies with a click. The quickbar puts spells, potions, scrolls, wands, activations and simple commands on the number keys, with Shift and Ctrl rows, and each character keeps its own slots. The rest panel answers the game's rest prompt, shows how long a rest has left and can stop it. While you aim a ball or breath, the blast preview shades the tiles it would reach. Each has its own switch and starts off.
- [Visible] [UI] **An item panel for your pack, equipment, quiver and the floor.** It lists what you carry, wear and stand on, shows the game's own description of the selected item in sections you can fold, and compares it with each slot it could go in. It answers the game's quantity and item questions with buttons, marks new items, and offers buttons to wield, drop, inscribe, use or ignore the selected item. Your ignore settings and auto-inscriptions can be changed from the panel too. Each part has its own switch and starts off.
- [Visible] [UI] **Click to walk, a right-click menu for each tile, and a gold aiming path.** A left-click steps or travels to a tile, attacks a creature next to you, or picks the target while you aim. Shift-click a tile next to you to run that way, and Ctrl-click any tile to make it your target. A right-click lists what you can do on that tile. While you aim, a gold line shows where the shot would go. Each has its own switch and starts off.
- [Visible] [UI] **Zoom and pan, hover cards and a full-level map overview, brought over from Quality of Life.** Zoom keeps whole cells so tiles stay crisp, and it can also follow your character, pan with a mouse drag, keep the aiming cursor on screen and centre on each new level. Hover cards work on the dungeon view and the map overview with one shared delay. The map overview adds an outline of the main view, landmark markers with a legend, mouse zoom and pan, and a flat-colour schematic mode. Each feature has its own switch and starts off.
- [Visible] [UI] **High-contrast and colourblind displays, first-encounter alerts, an itemized quiver and sharper shrunken tiles, brought over from Quality of Life.** Each has its own switch and starts off. The two display filters also cover this mod's panels. If a Quality of Life version from before the move is also on, the AnybandUI switches for these, zoom and hover cards stay off, so nothing is drawn twice.
- [Visible] [UI] **Panels for the character column, the status line and the message line.** Each has its own switch on the Mods screen and starts off. The character card shows bars for hit points, spell points, food and experience beside your stats, gold, armour and speed, and on a game version with movable panels it is a panel of its own. The status panel shows a badge for each timed effect, a card for the current level, and a badge while the Borg or another mod is playing for you. The message log folds repeated messages, can be searched, and carries on past a -more- pause when clicked.
- [Internal] **The mod repository, with a plugin that loads and adds nothing to the screen.** It declares no capabilities and logs one line when the game loads it.
